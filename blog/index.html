The ideas behind every box

One article per building block you place in the game, in the order the levels need them. Read one before a level, or after it to see what the simulation was modelling.

  1. 01

    Back-of-the-envelope estimation

    Level 1, 2, 5

    Before you draw a single box, work out how much traffic, data and bandwidth you are designing for.

  2. 02

    Load balancing and horizontal scaling

    Level 2, 6

    Add machines instead of buying bigger ones, and put something in front that spreads the work and routes around failures.

  3. 03

    Caching

    Level 3, 5, 6, 7

    Keep the answers to popular questions in memory so the database only sees the questions nobody asked recently.

  4. 04

    Database replication

    Level 3, 6

    Copies of your database give you more read capacity and a standby for when the primary dies. They cost consistency.

  5. 05

    CDNs and edge caching

    Level 4, 7

    You can't make light faster, but you can shorten the distance by serving bytes from a city near each user.

  6. 06

    Message queues and backpressure

    Level 5

    Accept work now, do it at a pace you can sustain, and make sure you eventually catch up.

  7. 07

    Availability, redundancy and failover

    Level 6, 2

    Every component fails eventually. Design so that one failure costs you capacity, not the whole site.

  8. 08

    Sharding and consistent hashing

    Level 7

    When one primary can't absorb your writes, split the data so each database owns a slice.
